## The arithmetic

| | |
|---|---|
| Cost per x402 transaction on XRPL | **$0.0002** |
| Cost to manufacture 1,000,000 payments | **~$200** |
| Total fees across 1.4M real settled XRPL payments | **~$280** |

A metric that costs $200 to move by a million units is not measuring demand. It is
measuring willingness to spend $200.

## What gets reported vs. what is true

<Steps>
  <Step title="A protocol launches and reports transaction counts">
    Counts rise fast. Press coverage follows. The number is real — the transactions
    genuinely settled on-chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Nobody separates commerce from testing">
    Integration suites, demos, load tests, and looping agents all produce identical
    on-chain artefacts to real purchases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Valuations and roadmaps get built on the number">
    x402 carried a multi-billion-dollar ecosystem valuation against roughly $28,000 in
    daily volume, much of it publicly described as testing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Value diversity gives it away">
    Real billing produces computed amounts. Loops produce round ones. This is trivially
    measurable and nobody was measuring it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to measure instead

<Card title="Value diversity" icon="chart-simple">
  Distinct payment amounts ÷ payments. Metered billing charges `0.020325` because that is
  what 6,775 tokens cost. A loop charges `0.001` because someone typed it.

  **XRPL: 3 distinct values across 156 payments. Base: 340 across 842.**
</Card>

The other three signals — payer diversity, failure presence, tail presence — are covered
in [the Authenticity Score](/authenticity-score).

<Tip>
This is not an argument that x402 is fake. Base shows genuinely real metered commerce.
It is an argument that **counts cannot distinguish the two**, and until now nothing did.
</Tip>
