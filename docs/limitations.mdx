---
title: "Limitations"
description: "Where this tool is wrong, stated plainly."
---

A measurement tool that hides its error bars is worthless. These are ours.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sampling, not census" icon="clock">
    A scan reads a short recent window — roughly 2–10 minutes. Daily figures extrapolate
    that window across 24 hours.

    **Order of magnitude, not accounting.** Diurnal and weekly patterns are invisible in a
    single sample. Use `x402scan watch` on a schedule for anything trend-shaped.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Small samples inflate the score" icon="chart-line">
    | Sample | Base score |
    |---|---|
    | 40 units | 81 |
    | 60 units | 89 |
    | 250 units | **71** |

    The direction is stable — Base always beats XRPL by a wide margin — but the absolute
    number needs **250+ units** before it is quotable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Base failure rate is unmeasurable" icon="eye-slash">
    Payments are decoded from block calldata, which cannot reveal reverts. Reported as
    `null` and omitted from scoring rather than guessed. Receipt sampling would fix it at
    the cost of one RPC call per payment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Public RPCs throttle" icon="gauge-high">
    `xrplcluster.com` returns `FUP exceeded` under load. The scanner rotates endpoints and
    reports what it actually got. **Always compare `sampling.scanned` to
    `sampling.requested`** before trusting a result.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Facilitator pooling on XRPL" icon="share-nodes">
    On XRPL the submitter is the payer, so a facilitator relaying for many clients would
    compress the payer count. XRPL's nine payers may be relay accounts rather than nine end
    users.

    The value signals are unaffected — a median of 0.001 across three distinct amounts is
    damning regardless of who submitted it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Two chains" icon="link">
    XRPL and Base. Solana is the obvious next adapter and carries roughly 22% of x402
    volume. See [adding a chain](/chains/adding-a-chain).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
If a number from this tool is going somewhere that matters: run 250+ units, check the
sampling ratio, and take two samples hours apart.
</Tip>
