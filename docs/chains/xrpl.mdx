## What the scan found

<Warning>
**156 payments. Three distinct values.** `0.001`, `0.002`, `0.003` XRP. Nothing else.

Nine unique payers, each firing 17–18 payments in the same window — near-identical counts,
metronomic spacing. 100% success rate. Total value across all 156 payments: **$0.23**.

Extrapolated, the entire XRPL agentic economy moves roughly **$42/day**.
</Warning>

Published figures for the same period reported 1.4M+ cumulative transactions and ~1,064
payments/hour. **Both are accurate.** The rate reproduced exactly in our scan. The counts
are simply not measuring commerce.

## Deterministic finality

XRPL settles in 3–5 seconds with no reorgs, so the scanner needs no confirmation depth or
rollback handling. XRPL is the *easier* chain to instrument — it just has little to
instrument yet.

## The open question

<Note>
Nine payers is not proof of nine users. In a facilitator model, t54 may submit settlements
from a pool of relay accounts on behalf of many clients.

The value signals are unaffected: a median of 0.001 XRP across three distinct amounts with
zero failures is a test-harness signature regardless of who submitted it.
</Note>

## Why it still matters

XRPL has on-ledger compliance primitives no other major chain offers — Credentials (live
Sept 2025) and Permissioned Domains (live Feb 2026). When institutional agentic volume
arrives, that infrastructure is already there. Today it is not being used.
