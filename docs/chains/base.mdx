## The honest counterweight

<Warning>
Recipient concentration is **worse** than XRPL's: top recipient 42%, top two 83%, across
just 34 recipients. "The Base x402 market" is substantially two companies.

That is a normal early-marketplace shape — many buyers, few sellers — but the addressable
list of services today is very short.
</Warning>

Total settled value is roughly **$10,000/day** ecosystem-wide, or ~$3.6M/year. Real, and
still small.

## Payer identity

EIP-3009 carries the payer inside the signed authorization, so `from` is the **actual
spender** even though a facilitator submits the transaction. This is why Base payer counts
are more trustworthy than XRPL's.

## Endpoints

Defaults to `mainnet.base.org` and `base-rpc.publicnode.com`, rotating on failure.
