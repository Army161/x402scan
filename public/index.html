Is agentic payment volume real, or a test harness?

x402 transactions cost about $0.0002, so counting them measures enthusiasm, not commerce. This measures whether settlement behaves like real metered billing — live, from the chains, right now.

Press “Run live scan” to measure both chains.

How the score works

Four signals a test harness cannot cheaply fake. Signals that cannot be measured on a chain are omitted and the remaining weights renormalised — a chain is never penalised for our instrumentation gap.

SignalWeightWhat it catches
Value diversity40%Metered billing produces computed amounts like 0.020325. Loops produce 0.001.
Payer diversity25%Real demand comes from many wallets, unevenly.
Failure presence15%Real commerce fails 1–5%. A flawless success rate is a controlled environment.
Tail presence20%Real distributions have outliers. If max ≈ p95, nobody buys anything unusual.

Bands — 70+ commerce · 45–69 mixed · 25–44 mostly synthetic · <25 test harness. Below 20 observed payments, no score is returned.

Caveat, stated plainly: each scan samples a short recent window (roughly 2–10 minutes) and extrapolates daily figures from it. That is order of magnitude, not accounting. Small samples move the score — a 60-unit Base scan read 89 where a 250-unit scan read 71. Diurnal and weekly patterns are invisible in a single sample.

Public RPC endpoints throttle under load. Always check sampling.scanned against sampling.requested before quoting a number.
